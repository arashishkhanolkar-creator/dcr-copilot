How this works

Upload any .dxf file — every layer it contains shows up on the right with its own line colour, weight, line type and opacity, plus a fill colour and opacity wherever that layer has closed shapes.

Hatching needs a closed loop. A shape only fills with colour if its outline forms one unbroken loop — a closed polyline, or lines and arcs that connect exactly end to end with no gaps or overlaps. Anything left open renders as an outline only, so if a fill isn't showing up, check that boundary is fully closed before raising its Fill opacity.
