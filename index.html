About Designhaus

Designhaus Collective is a multidisciplinary design studio creating thoughtful spaces, compelling visual experiences, and innovative ideas at the intersection of design, technology, and culture. In addition to spatial design, we engage in AI research and explore emerging creative tools to deliver distinctive, purposeful, and contemporary work.

Work

Architecture

We design thoughtful spaces from the ground up, balancing context, function, and architectural character. From concept to construction, every detail is considered.

Interior Design

Interiors shaped through material, proportion, light, and detail. We create considered environments that feel cohesive, tactile, and distinctly their own.

3D Visualization

We turn design intent into compelling visual experiences. Precise, atmospheric imagery built from real design information—not generic stock assets.

Branding & Graphic Design

We build identities that extend beyond the logo into a cohesive visual language. Thoughtful systems designed for studios, spaces, and brands with a connection to the built world.

AI & Design Tools

We explore how AI can make design work smarter, faster, and more capable. From internal tools to intelligent workflows, we build technology around the way designers actually work.
