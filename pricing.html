Before you start a trial

What happens after my 7-day trial?

Your trial gives full access to whichever plan you pick — Designer or Practice. If you don't subscribe by day 7, access is paused until you do. No charge happens automatically; the trial genuinely requires no card.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes — you can move between Designer and Practice at any time from your account.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep full access until the end of your current billing period, then your subscription ends — no partial refunds, no further charges.

Is Feasibility Studio really unlimited on Practice?

Yes — unlimited sites/projects, with full persistent conversation history across all of them.
